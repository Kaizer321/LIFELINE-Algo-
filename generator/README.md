## Generator conventions

- Waves are numbered from 0.
- Tour steps and pipeline indexes are numbered from 0.
- Radio parts are numbered from 1; windows, alarms, safety rules, and crates start at 1.
- Touching windows do not count as overlapping.
- Trap 49 measures repetition as agreement with the most common block of at most 8 symbols at 90% or more of positions.
- Nearest-neighbour tour ties are resolved by the lowest site number.
